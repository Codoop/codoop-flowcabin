# IP Originality Gate

Use this gate whenever a creator names, supplies, or requests something that
could identify a third-party franchise, character, brand, artist, game, film,
or show. This is practical creative guidance, not a legal clearance opinion.

## Decide and redirect

1. Identify the creator's underlying goal: genre, mood, gameplay fantasy,
   audience, or broad visual era.
2. Exclude identifiable expression: names, logos, catchphrases, character
   likenesses, distinctive silhouettes, costume/prop combinations, symbols,
   title treatments, signature locations, and recreations of a known image or
   composition.
3. Invent at least three independent replacements across world premise,
   protagonist/role, silhouette and materials, palette, motifs, UI language,
   mechanics, or story. Do not merely rename the same design.
4. Write the desired abstraction, exclusions, and replacements in
   `visual-direction.md` before generating any asset or image prompt.

Say briefly: “I can make an original game/artwork with the same broad
adventure, cozy, heroic, or sci-fi feeling, but not a recognizable copy of that
IP.” Then proceed with the distinct concept; do not ask the creator to make a
legal determination unless licensing is genuinely required.

## Image-prompt rules

Describe only the original concept. Never use a named IP, “in the style of,”
“lookalike,” “almost identical,” or a request to reproduce a supplied image.
Specify the new subject, setting, shapes, materials, palette, lighting, and
composition. If the generated result is still recognizable as the referenced
IP, regenerate with more separation before using it.

## Examples

- Preserve: “a collectible-creature exploration game.” Replace: a named
  creature roster, ball-shaped capture device, familiar type symbols, and a
  matching trainer outfit with original ecology, bonding mechanics, equipment,
  and silhouettes.
- Preserve: “a dark space opera with laser-sword tension.” Replace: named
  factions, iconic helmet/robe silhouettes, weapon designs, and signature
  locations with a new political conflict, toolset, materials, and visual
  language.
- Preserve: “a cheerful Italian-style platformer.” Replace: the plumber hero,
  mushroom/brick iconography, enemy shapes, and level compositions with a new
  traversal fantasy, cast, terrain grammar, and UI motifs.
